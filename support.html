Help & support

Questions, a wrong rate, or a bug? Email parksg.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Tell us your iPhone model and the app version (Settings in ParkSG, at the bottom).

How does ParkSG know I parked?

While you drive, ParkSG uses your iPhone's motion sensor and a low-power location session. When the drive ends at a carpark, and your phone leaves the car's Bluetooth or CarPlay (or you walk away), it checks which carpark you are in. If it is sure, your Lock Screen asks "Parked?". Stops away from carparks, such as home, stay silent.

Why does it need location "Always"?

Parking usually happens while the app is closed. With "Always", iOS can wake ParkSG when a drive ends so it can find the carpark and start the timer. Without it, the map, rates and live lots still work, and you can tap "I've parked here" yourself.

Is the fare exact?

It is an estimate. HDB rates come from HDB's published charges and include the 15-minute grace period, daily and night caps, and free parking on Sundays and public holidays where it applies. Mall rates come from the rates LTA lists on OneMotoring and can be out of date. The charge at the carpark is always final. If a rate looks wrong, open the carpark in ParkSG and tap "Rates wrong? Tell us".

It picked the wrong carpark

Open the parking card and tap "Wrong carpark" to choose the right one; the fare updates straight away. You can also change the start time.

A ride in a taxi or bus ended my session

ParkSG only ends a session when it sees your own car again (its Bluetooth or CarPlay), or when you start driving from where you parked. If it gets it wrong, end or adjust the session from the parking card, and let us know.

Home or season parking

While parked there, turn on "No charge here" on the parking card. ParkSG then stays quiet at that carpark.

Battery

ParkSG uses precise GPS only while you drive. Between drives it relies on low-power location changes and the motion sensor, which use very little battery.

Turning things off

In ParkSG, open Settings (the profile button) to turn off parking detection or the Lock Screen timer. You can also change location, motion and notification access in the iPhone Settings app.
