Help & support

Questions, a wrong rate, or a bug? Email parksg.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Tell us your iPhone model and the app version (Settings in ParkSG, at the bottom).

How does ParkSG know I parked?

You set up two automations in Apple's Shortcuts app, once. When your iPhone disconnects from your car's Bluetooth, ParkSG checks which carpark you are in. If it is sure, your Lock Screen asks "Parked?". When the phone connects to the car again, the session ends. Stops away from carparks, such as home, stay silent. Step-by-step setup guide with pictures.

Why does it need location "Always"?

Parking usually happens while the app is closed. With "Always", ParkSG can read your location when the automation runs in the background, so it can find the carpark and start the timer. Without it, the map, rates and live lots still work, and you can tap "I've parked here" yourself.

Is the fare exact?

It is an estimate. HDB rates come from HDB's published charges and include the 15-minute grace period, daily and night caps, and free parking on Sundays and public holidays where it applies. Mall rates come from the rates LTA lists on OneMotoring and can be out of date. The charge at the carpark is always final. If a rate looks wrong, open the carpark in ParkSG and tap "Rates wrong? Tell us".

It picked the wrong carpark

Open the parking card and tap "Wrong carpark" to choose the right one; the fare updates straight away. You can also change the start time.

A session ended when it shouldn't have

ParkSG ends a session when your phone connects to your own car again. If you turned on "Detect without the automation", a drive in another vehicle from where you parked can also end it; turn that off in ParkSG's Settings if it gets in the way. You can end or adjust a session from the parking card.

Home or season parking

While parked there, turn on "No charge here" on the parking card. ParkSG then stays quiet at that carpark.

Battery

With the car automations, ParkSG does nothing between drives: it wakes for a few seconds when your phone connects to or disconnects from the car. The optional "Detect without the automation" setting uses the motion sensor and low-power location, and GPS while you drive.

Turning things off

To stop parking detection, switch off the two automations in the Shortcuts app. In ParkSG, open Settings (the profile button) to turn off the Lock Screen timer. You can also change location and notification access in the iPhone Settings app.
