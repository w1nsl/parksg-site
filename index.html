ParkSG

Park, and know what you owe.

ParkSG is an iPhone app for Singapore drivers. It shows carparks near you with live lots and rates, notices when you park, and keeps the time and the amount due on your Lock Screen until you drive off.

  • Live lots for HDB, URA and mall carparks
  • Rates now, and cheaper carparks a short walk away
  • Starts when you leave the car, with a Lock Screen timer and fare
  • A reminder to start Parking.sg at coupon carparks
  • ERP charges nearby when you drive off

ParkSG is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HDB, URA, LTA, GovTech or Parking.sg. Fares are estimates.
